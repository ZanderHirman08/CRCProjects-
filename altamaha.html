The Little Amazon

The Altamaha River delta in near-infrared

The Altamaha drains about a quarter of Georgia and is never dammed along its main stem. Before it reaches the sea at Altamaha Sound it spreads into a maze of cypress–tupelo swamp, tidal freshwater forest and salt marsh. It is one of the largest undeveloped river deltas on the Atlantic coast.

In this false-color view the satellite's near-infrared band is drawn as red. Healthy leaves reflect near-infrared strongly, so the more alive the land is, the redder it glows. Drag the handle to see the same place in ordinary true color.
